The method

The SEEDER Framework™

A living, cyclical method for observing human experience, identifying the conditions shaping it, designing more aligned conditions, testing them in lived reality, and returning value back into the system.

Core proposition

Stop endlessly correcting the human downstream. Go upstream and cultivate the conditions.

SEEDER is not a command-and-control method. It assumes living systems have capacities of their own. The work is to see clearly, understand what is shaping the experience, and create conditions in which life can reveal what it is capable of.

The regenerative standard

A system can be productive and still be extractive.

SEEDER asks what participation does to the life inside the system—not only whether the system produces an output.

Does participation increase capacity or consume it?

Applications

Different settings. The same design question.

What conditions would allow the people inside this system to participate with greater dignity, agency, capacity, relationship, and regenerative value?

01

Families + homes

Spaces, routines, roles, access, and expectations designed around the actual people living there.

02

Workplaces

Workflows, schedules, tools, culture, and management conditions that support human capacity rather than chronic depletion.

03

Education

Learning environments designed around participation, development, family life, and access rather than institutional habit alone.

04

Technology

Interfaces and digital systems whose structures embody the human behaviors and relationships they claim to support.

05

Organizations + community

Structures where mission, resources, participation, decision-making, and lived experience are brought into alignment.

06

Economic systems

Models that examine how contribution creates value and how value can circulate back toward the conditions that sustain life.

About Sheng Lee

I design better systems for everyday life.

I look at how people are living, working, or functioning, figure out what is not working for them, and design a better way for it to work.

My work sits at the intersection of family life, organizational culture, technology, business, space, human development, economics, and everyday participation.

I do not begin by assuming the person is the problem. I look at what the surrounding environment is rewarding, requiring, preventing, or quietly teaching—and whether the system itself needs to change.

Go See Creator is where that way of seeing becomes a body of work, a method, and a set of real-world experiments in better living conditions.
